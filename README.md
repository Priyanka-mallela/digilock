# DigiLock: Project Report

## 1. Abstract

DigiLock is a secure digital locker built to give people one safe place for their important documents. Instead of hunting through chats, emails and old folders every time an ID or certificate is needed, users can keep everything in a single space that only they can open. This report walks through why DigiLock was built, how it works, how it protects data, and where it could go next.

## 2. Introduction

Think about the last time you needed an important document in a hurry. Chances are it was buried in a WhatsApp chat, an old email, or a folder you forgot you made. Most of us treat IDs, certificates, bank statements and contracts pretty casually, even though losing or leaking them can cause real trouble.

DigiLock started from that simple frustration. The goal was a place that is easy enough for anyone to use and secure enough to actually trust.

## 3. The Problem

- Important documents end up scattered across many places, so finding one takes far longer than it should.
- Files sent over chats and email can be seen by people they were never meant for.
- Paper copies get lost, damaged or simply forgotten.
- Most people don't have a single protected space that belongs only to them.

## 4. What We Set Out to Do

1. Give users one secure home for all their documents.
2. Make sure only the account owner can get in.
3. Keep uploading, finding and downloading quick and painless.
4. Keep everything simple enough that anyone can use it without a tutorial.

## 5. How It Works

Using DigiLock is meant to feel effortless:

1. **Sign up and log in.** You create an account and sign in with your own credentials.
2. **Upload your documents.** Add files from your device in a few clicks.
3. **Stay protected.** Your files are stored securely, so they aren't sitting around in plain view.
4. **Get them back anytime.** View, download or delete any document whenever you like.

## 6. Keeping Things Secure

Security isn't an extra in DigiLock, it's the whole point. So a few principles guide everything:

- Only signed-in users can reach their locker.
- Passwords are never stored as plain text.
- Stored files are protected with encryption.
- Every user sees only their own documents, nobody else's.

## 7. Why It Helps

- **It's convenient.** Everything lives in one place and is there when you need it.
- **It's private.** Your documents stay out of chats and inboxes.
- **It gives peace of mind.** There's far less worry about losing or leaking something important.
- **You're in control.** You decide what stays and what goes.

## 8. Where It Falls Short (For Now)

- The locker is only as safe as the password you choose, so users need to look after their login details.
- You need an internet connection to reach your files.
- Some handy extras, like secure sharing and account recovery options, haven't been added yet.

## 9. What's Next

There's a lot of room to grow. Some ideas on the list:

- Two-factor authentication for an extra layer of protection
- Shareable links that expire after a set time
- Folders and tags to keep things neatly organized
- A mobile app for access on the go
- Reminders for documents about to expire, like licenses and insurance policies

## 10. Conclusion

DigiLock solves a small but very real everyday problem: keeping important documents safe and easy to find. With a simple design and secure storage at its core, it gives people a dependable place for the files that matter most, and there's plenty of room for it to grow into something even more useful.
